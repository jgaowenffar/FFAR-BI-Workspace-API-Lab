Reading the cost result

Costs use reported input, cached input, output and web-search calls. Reasoning tokens are already included in output. Unknown charges remain unknown; a known subtotal is not a complete cost. Database/hosting fees and regional or long-context uplifts are excluded. A low bill does not prove useful research: inspect coverage, facts, sources and financial periods alongside it.
